    How the agent works

    Six steps, no guesswork. Every step leaves a record you can check.

    1. Scan the nicheFinds videos in your niche with brand integrations from the last 90 days.
    2. Identify sponsorsLists the brands behind those integrations — brands already paying creators like you.
    3. Verify activityDrops brands whose sponsorships expired, and flags reused or misleading clips.
    4. Find the contactLocates the partnerships / creator contact and the decision-maker where public.
    5. Draft outreachWrites a personal email per brand: your numbers, why the fit is real, one clear ask.
    6. Follow upSchedules one polite follow-up per brand and reports replies weekly.

    What you get

    Verified brand list

    10–20 brands already sponsoring channels your size, with the evidence: which video, when, what deal type.

    Verified contacts

    The right inbox per brand — partnerships, creator, or influencer contact — checked before outreach.

    Personalised drafts

    One outreach email per brand, written around your channel's numbers and the brand's actual campaigns.

    Follow-up & report

    One follow-up per brand, plus a weekly report: sent, replied, negotiating, closed.
